# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Leads / Solution Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads or Solution Architects guide technical design and integration so the implementation is coherent, feasible, and aligned with project goals.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate technical design and document significant architecture and integration decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks early
- Support Developers with implementation choices and cross-component coordination
- Contribute technical estimates and advise on trade-offs

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Product Manager to understand product needs and clarify technical implications of acceptance criteria
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, risks, estimates, and delivery impacts
- Guide and support Developers without replacing their implementation ownership
- Work with QA/Testing to make designs testable and address integration risks
- Explain technical options and trade-offs to Stakeholders in accessible terms

---

## UX/UI Designers and Researchers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers bring user needs and usability evidence into discovery and delivery, shaping experiences that are useful and understandable.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct or synthesize user research and identify usability needs
- Create design artifacts, interaction flows, and design guidance
- Define usability considerations and acceptance criteria with the product team
- Validate designs with users or other appropriate feedback methods

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to connect user evidence and design choices to product goals and priorities
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on research, design milestones, and delivery dependencies
- Work with Developers to clarify designs, accessibility needs, and feasible implementation details
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate usability and design acceptance criteria
- Gather relevant context and feedback from Stakeholders while keeping user needs central

---

## Data / Analytics Leads

### Role Summary
Data / Analytics Leads make sure project outcomes can be measured with reliable data and that teams can use evidence to evaluate delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into instrumentation and measurement requirements
- Define analytics events, reporting needs, and data-quality checks
- Identify privacy and data dependencies in measurement plans
- Analyze results and communicate limitations and findings

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to define outcome measures and interpret product results
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on measurement dependencies, reporting timelines, and risks
- Partner with Developers to implement instrumentation and with QA/Testing to validate events, data quality, and reports
- Share findings and caveats with Stakeholders to support evidence-informed decisions

---

## Security / Privacy Leads

### Role Summary
Security / Privacy Leads advise teams on protecting systems and data and on meeting applicable security and privacy expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Identify security, privacy, and compliance risks and recommend appropriate controls
- Advise on threat modeling, data handling, and secure design
- Help define security and privacy requirements and validation activities
- Support triage of security or privacy concerns and recommend escalation

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Developers and QA/Testing to incorporate and validate security and privacy controls
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to record risks, track mitigations, and escalate unresolved concerns
- Advise the Product Manager on impacts to scope, user experience, and product decisions
- Provide stakeholders, including the Product Lead or sponsor when needed, with risk context for decisions; the lead advises, while accountable project leadership determines priorities and accepts risks

---

## Operations / Support Representatives

### Role Summary
Operations / Support Representatives prepare for reliable service operation and ensure support teams can respond to customer needs after delivery.

### Responsibilities
- Identify operational, monitoring, deployment, and support-readiness requirements
- Contribute runbooks, alerting needs, and service handoff information
- Surface operational risks and recurring customer or incident feedback
- Participate in release readiness and post-release verification as appropriate

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on release dependencies, readiness, handoffs, and stakeholder communications
- Work with Developers on operability, monitoring, deployment, and incident response needs
- Partner with QA/Testing to include operational and post-deployment checks in validation
- Share support trends and customer feedback with the Product Manager to inform priorities
- Communicate support readiness and known issues to relevant Stakeholders

---

## Assigning Roles

These roles describe functions and areas of accountability, not necessarily separate positions. Assign or combine them according to project size and context, while making clear who owns each responsibility and decision.
